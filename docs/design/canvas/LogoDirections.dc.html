Directions, low fidelity Three ways to solidify the same creature. A is what the brand sheet and the app use; B and C are here in case the sheep should read louder.
A. Solid chosen
Why: The quietest silhouette: cloud, two faces, nothing else. Closest to Docker, Linear and Vercel marks. Tradeoff: At 16px the eyes go and it reads as a cloud with two nubs; the wordmark carries the name.
B. Layered
Why: A halo separates each head from the fleece, so the heads read as faces in front of the body. Tradeoff: The halo is a second layer to maintain on every background, and it muddies below 32px.
C. Grounded
Why: Two legs make it an animal at a glance, even as a favicon. Tradeoff: Less abstract, more mascot; it sits lower and squarer in a lockup.
